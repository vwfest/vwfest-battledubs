How to play

  1. Park your fleet on the 10×10 lot: a Samba Bus (5), Kombi Bus (4), Karmann Ghia (3), Beetle (3) and Baby Bug (2). Tap a dub, tap the lot, rotate with R.
  2. Take turns launching mortars. Pick a square on the enemy lot. A miss leaves a pothole. A hit sets a dub on fire.
  3. Knock out every dub before they get yours. 30 seconds a turn.

Fair play: fleets stay on the server, so nobody can peek. Disconnect and you have 20 seconds to come back before you forfeit. Repeat games between the same two players stop counting after a few per day.
